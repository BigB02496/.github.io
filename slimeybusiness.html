Slimey Business

Game Programmer - Personal Project

August, 2024 Game Programmer

Summary

  • Type: Personal Project
  • Completed: Unreleased
  • Acquirable: No
  • My Role: Game Programmer
  • Time Frame: 2 months

Overview

This was my first truly solo project. I aimed to create a wave based first person shooter where the objective was to survive waves of "slime" enemies with increasing difficulty.

Project Breakdown

The player spawned in a confined room with only a door blocking their path. Points were rewarded to the player for succesful shots and eliminations of enemies.

The player could spend their points to open doors or buy upgrades.

Multiple enemy types would spawn giving a variety of challenge for the player.

Concepts learned

As a solo project I learned:

  • How to use the Unity Engines navmesh system to program enemy AI.
  • How to use Unity Engines particle system for weapon and enemy effects.
  • How to overcome the challenges that come with a first person character controller.

My Role

I was responsible for the following work:

  • Gameplay systems and architecture
  • Performance and optimization
  • UI programming
  • Animations
  • Level Design

What I would do next time

If I were to revist or expand upon title I would:

  • Add a variety of weapons
  • More variation to enemies
  • Improved enemy AI and pathing
