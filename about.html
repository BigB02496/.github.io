About Me

Systems and technical programming

Hi, my name is Charles Fache. I'm an aspiring game programmer with a passion for creating unique experiences for gamers of every community.

I've worked on game programming as a hobby for many years and as of 2025 went to Front Range Community College to get my degree in programming. I even made this website during my time there.

In my spare time, I am a huge gamer and nerd overall. I enjoy a wide variety of games though my favorites are RPG's like Skyrim and Witcher 3. My wife and I frequent conventions like FanExpo and often cosplay for them. I hope to bring this energy to any projects I take part of.
