Overview

This was my first learning experience with game programming. It was a heavily guided processes that taught me about game engines and how to get desired behaviors from the game engine through code.

Project Breakdown

Recreating the game "Overcooked"

The ultimate end result of this project was to recreate mechanics used in Ghost Town Games title Overcooked.

The project was a simple slice of what Overcooked actually was. In this demo, the player was given one of four recipes, and it was their job to make them in order. Either a salad, plain burger, cheeseburger, or deluxe burger.

I learned how to handle a character controller, how to get the character to interact with the environment and event handlers.

Core Gameplay Concept

Players serve food by using:

  • A stovetop with 3 stages for the burger pattys (uncooked, cooked, burnt)
  • A cutting board to cut cheese, lettuce, and tomatoes.
  • A serving convery that checked the accuracy of the recipe.
  • A trashcan to dispose of recipes that were incorrect or no longer needed.

All together, it created a fast paced demo similar to Ghost Town Games title Overcooked

My Role

This was a solo project for a class project. The art and assets were provided to me by my instructor. Work by me included:

  • Gameplay systems and architecture
  • Performance and optimization

What I would do next time

If I were to revist or expand upon title I would:

  • Make a few more levels with differing challenges.
  • Add unique mechanics to seperate it from similar titles.
